Greg Brockman

Greg Brockman is the mastermind behind many of OpenAI's innovations. Born in 1988 in the United States, Brockman studied Computer Science at Harvard University before working at Stripe, a company that became one of the largest online payment systems in the world. Brockman was among the first to recognize the potential of AI in transforming industries.

In 2015, Greg Brockman decided to leave Stripe to join OpenAI as a co-founder. He became the CTO of the company and was responsible for leading engineering teams that worked on GPT and OpenAI's language models.

Brockman played a crucial role in developing the core technologies that have made OpenAI one of the most advanced companies in artificial intelligence. He is also a strong advocate for creating AI that benefits humanity as a whole, not just a select few.
