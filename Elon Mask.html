Elon Mask

Elon Musk is one of the most famous personalities in technology and innovation. Born in South Africa in 1971, Musk has always been interested in technological advancements and the future. He started his journey by founding Zip2, later sold, and then went on to create PayPal and SpaceX. He became the CEO of Tesla, changing the electric vehicle industry forever.

However, Elon Musk's story with OpenAI began in 2015. He was deeply concerned about the future of artificial intelligence. He feared that if AI was developed irresponsibly, it could pose a threat to humanity. So, he joined a group of like-minded pioneers to found OpenAI, with the mission to develop safe and advanced AI for the benefit of all mankind.

Musk was a major early supporter, but he withdrew from the OpenAI Board of Directors in 2018 due to conflict of interest with his work at Tesla, which was also developing AI for self-driving cars. Despite leaving the board, Musk continued to be vocal about AI safety concerns and remained one of the industry's most prominent critics.
