Sam Altman

Sam Altman is one of the leading figures in the tech world. Born in 1985 in the United States, Altman developed an interest in internet technologies early on. He founded Loopt, a mobile location-based service, which was later acquired in 2012. Following this, Altman joined Y Combinator, one of Silicon Valley's most successful startup incubators.

In 2015, Altman became one of the founding members of OpenAI. He played a pivotal role in transitioning OpenAI from a non-profit organization into a for-profit capped entity. Altman’s mission was to ensure that AI benefits humanity safely and responsibly. He soon became the CEO of OpenAI.

During his leadership, OpenAI achieved monumental breakthroughs, such as the launch of GPT-3 and ChatGPT, which propelled the company to the forefront of the AI industry and made it a household name in tech.
